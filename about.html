About AWLegacy

Our Family Legacy

A parent company for the Kurzeja family, built to own operating businesses and real estate with a long horizon.

Who we are

A family platform for work we intend to hold

AWLegacy is the Kurzeja family’s investing and real estate business, owned and led by Amanda Kurzeja, Owner, and the parent company we are building for our family.

We are organizing AWLegacy so that operating companies, real estate, and future ventures can sit under one family parent, with clear ownership and a long-term mandate.

How we intend to operate

These are principles, not performance claims.

Operator-led

We prefer businesses where experienced operators are close to the work itself — including, where we are involved, members of our own family.

Patient capital

We would rather own something worthwhile for a long time than chase a quick outcome. Decisions should still make sense for the next generation.

Stewardship

Partners, employees, customers, and the communities around our businesses deserve straight dealing and a company that takes care of what it owns.
