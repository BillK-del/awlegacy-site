How we work

A holding company, not a short-term trade

AWLegacy is being built as a family parent company. Our aim is to own operating businesses and the real estate that supports them, and to hold those assets with a multi-generational outlook.

Operating businesses

We intend to own and help build companies in industries we understand, including selected service businesses.

Real estate

Where it makes sense, we look to own the real estate that supports our operating companies, and to hold other property with the same long view.

Long-term ownership

We are not organized as a publicly advertised fund. We prefer durable ownership, aligned partners, and decisions that still make sense a generation from now.
